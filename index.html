How It Works

From inbox
to interview
in minutes.

HireGrid connects to your ATS or email inbox, reads every resume the moment it arrives, and delivers a ranked shortlist before you've finished your morning coffee.

01 / 05
📥
Upload / Connect

Drop a folder of resumes, connect your ATS (Greenhouse, Lever, Workday), or forward your hiring inbox. HireGrid ingests everything instantly.

02 / 05
🧠
AI Reads All

Our LLM reads every resume — PDFs, DOCs, scanned images — and extracts 47 structured signals per candidate including skills, gaps, and red flags.

Score & Rank

Each candidate receives a fit score (0–100) based on your exact job requirements. Custom weights for skills, experience, education, and culture signals.

HireGrid drafts personalized, empathetic rejection emails for every passed candidate — with one-click send or schedule. Never ghost another applicant.

05 / 05
📅
You Interview

Review your ranked shortlist of top 10 candidates with AI-generated summaries and suggested interview questions. You only meet the best.
